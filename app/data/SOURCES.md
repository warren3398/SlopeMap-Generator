# Land and shoreline sources

Philippine land: OCHA Philippines / National Mapping and Resource Information Authority, distributed through geoBoundaries gbOpen PHL ADM0, revision 9469f09.
- Source: https://www.geoboundaries.org/api/current/gbOpen/PHL/ADM0/
- Data: https://github.com/wmgeolab/geoBoundaries/tree/9469f09/releaseData/gbOpen/PHL/ADM0
- License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 IGO, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/igo/

Surrounding land: Natural Earth 1:10 million land, public domain.
- Source: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/10m-physical-vectors/10m-land/
- Terms: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

The bundled derivative covers 100°E–140°E and 5°S–30°N. Philippine coastlines are generalized by approximately 22 meters; surrounding context is generalized for display. Coordinate precision is six decimal places. Inland holes are filled so missing hillshade and lakes are not classified as ocean. Natural Earth Philippine island components are replaced by the detailed Philippine land geometry. Internal administrative boundaries are dissolved. Attribution is printed on each JPEG.
