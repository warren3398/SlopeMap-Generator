GIS Shapefile Converter & Uploader

Import master slope polygons and Philippine administrative boundaries into the SlopeMap PostGIS database. Upload a ZIP containing .shp, .shx, .dbf and .prj, or a GeoJSON / GeoPackage file.

Select a file, then preview its attributes.
Geometry is reprojected to EPSG:4326, repaired if invalid, and imported transactionally. Each slope upload creates a new version; boundary uploads update matching codes. No existing slope datasets are deleted.
